This repository holds my Week 1 report for the YuvaIntern SAP MM Procurement Specialist internship, Strategic Procurement Planning in SAP MM. I develop a procurement plan for a hypothetical Indian steel fabrication workshop with about 25 people, annual turnover of about Rs 6 crore and approximately 30 suppliers of steel sections, plates, fasteners and paint. The case contains no real customer data.

I map the procurement cycle from material and supplier records through planning, sourcing, ordering, receipt, invoice verification, returns and payment. The report covers MM01, ME11, ME01, MRP through MD01 and MD04, purchase requisitions through ME51N, release strategy, RFQ and quotation comparison through ME41, ME47 and ME49, and purchase orders through ME21N. It also explains outline agreements, MIGO, MIRO, GR/IR and F110, alongside integration with FI, CO, SD, PP, QM and WM. Each stage is compared with Rigel ERP, which I build for small Indian traders and workshops.

The feature built in Rigel this week is three-way matching on the GRN. For a PO of 100 rods at Rs 50, 95 arrive while the supplier bills 100 at Rs 52, totaling Rs 5,200. The GRN value is Rs 4,940. It shows "Difference Rs 260: billed more than received" and a rate 4% above the PO's Rs 50. Owed remains Rs 4,940, payable in parts; the match warns without blocking.

The report includes measurable goals, a risk table, a 12-week rollout, staff hours, technology needs and a rupee budget comparing the supplied SAP Business One estimates with Rigel. It closes with KPIs, organizational alignment and Week 2 vendor evaluation.
