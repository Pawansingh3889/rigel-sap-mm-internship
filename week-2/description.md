This repository holds my Week 2 report for the YuvaIntern SAP MM Procurement Specialist internship, Vendor Evaluation and Selection in SAP MM. I continue the hypothetical Indian steel fabrication workshop case from Week 1: about 25 people, annual turnover of about Rs 6 crore and approximately 30 suppliers of steel sections, plates, fasteners and paint. The case contains no real customer data.

I explain purchasing info records through ME11, source lists through ME01, quota arrangements through MEQ1 and vendor evaluation through ME61, ME63, ME64 and ME65. The report covers price, quality, delivery and service criteria, their supporting evidence, weighting keys, approval and vendor blocking through XK05. I compare these SAP practices with Rigel ERP, which I build for small Indian traders and workshops.

The feature built in Rigel this week is the supplier score, live on Reports > Purchase > Suppliers. The matrix gives on time 40 points, fill rate 30, damage 20 and price 10. Shree Steels has 90% on time, 95% fill, 2% damage and a 3% price rise. Its points are 36 + 28.5 + 16 + 7 = 87.5, rounded to 88, grade A. Missing parts are excluded, and fewer than two parts with data means no score. The lowest scores appear first; the result blocks no supplier and changes no PO.

The report includes three worked examples, RFQ and quotation comparison, sample checks, trial orders, monthly supplier development, payment fairness, scoring risks, a 4 to 6 week rollout, staff hours, a rupee budget and KPIs. It closes with Week 3 master data management and damage reasons on GRN lines.
